# Design direction and review

The product's design quality is a primary requirement. Direction: the annotated paperback, with warm ivory, deep ink, restrained oxblood, literary serif content and quiet sans-serif controls. Reader writing leads; AI notes carry explicit labels and reduced emphasis. Dark mode uses warm text on charcoal. Avoid fake community data, ornamental clutter and a chat-bubble journal.

Before feature implementation, present Library, Journal, Ask and Gallery visual studies for mobile and desktop, including dark mode. Exact fonts are candidates until readability, licensing and loading are checked. The founder reviews the resulting screens before the next build phase.

Design all empty/loading/error/offline/saved states. Support keyboard use, focus visibility, screen-reader names, reduced motion, safe areas and enlarged text. Test long excerpts, missing covers, long book titles and no shared journals. Establish contrast and touch-target criteria in the foundation PR and check them in the implemented screens. PDFs require rendered visual review of short, long and multilingual content.

Reference research from the proposal: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) and [Radix accessibility](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/overview/accessibility). These guide interaction quality; they do not determine Marginalia's visual identity.
