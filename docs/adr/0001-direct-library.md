# ADR 0001: direct library onboarding

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: accepted direction.

The founder removed the reading-mode picker. After authentication and minimal identity setup, enter the library, where the reader adds their own book. Do not seed personal libraries automatically. Suggested books are optional and an empty library is valid. Literature, philosophy and literary nonfiction share the initial workflow. Other modes are deferred without onboarding placeholders.
