# ADR 0004: AI privacy and provider choice

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: pending live-provider approval.

The brief requests no training on reader data and also suggests a free Gemini tier. Those requirements may conflict. Proposed policy: synthetic examples on free development services; standard paid processing with suitable terms before real reader content. No paid account creation is authorized yet. Exact provider, stable model, retention notice, usage caps and retry behavior require verification before integration with real data. Keep provider interfaces and prompts versioned and backend-only. Do not claim no training or no retention without checking the active service terms.
