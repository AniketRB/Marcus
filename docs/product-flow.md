# Product flow

## Agreement and unresolved details

The founder approved moving toward development on 2026-11-08 after revising onboarding, adding the shared gallery to the MVP and choosing Render first with future Cloudflare portability. This document records that agreed direction. Defaults proposed by the builder, rather than explicitly settled by the founder, are identified below.

## First use

Sign in with email or Google, collect a display name, and enter the personal library. No use-mode picker and no automatically added books. An empty library is valid. An optional suggested-book list may be added once suitable catalog records are prepared; it is not required to begin. Selecting a suggestion creates the reader's own book record. A suggestion does not imply full-text availability.

## Read and capture

Add title and author; encourage translator, edition and language. Record optional page/chapter and status. Open the book's journal. Ask through text, voice or a photograph; alternatively create a manual post with text, photos, an editable voice transcript, quote, tags and reference.

For image Ask: extract text, let the reader correct it and choose the whole captured passage or selected lines, then explain the confirmed selection. The proposed default is to remove temporary Ask images after successful processing, subject to a short recovery window defined before implementation. Deliberate post attachments are separate private media.

An optional try-first interpretation precedes the AI answer. Answers separate plain meaning, book context, competing interpretations and evidenced translation notes. Label the actual grounding: excerpt, verified text, or general knowledge. Do not fabricate citations or assert authorial intent as fact. Reading position limits broader context; spoiler avoidance is best effort.

Save to the journal manually by default, with opt-in autosave. Proposed recent Ask history is private and deletable; retention must be specified before storing unsaved questions. Preserve reader writing and AI output in separate fields. Link reflections and explanations about the same passage.

## Journal and finish

Show chronological and page/chapter views, search, tags, editing and deletion. Treat capture time, book position and custom export ordering independently. Unplaced entries remain accessible. Capture drafts offline and synchronize explicitly when the application resumes/connectivity returns; do not depend on unsupported background behavior.

Finish with a closing reflection and honest statistics, then optionally curate and export. Reopening retains reading history. One editorial PDF template supports A5 and A4 with cover, dates, statistics, entries, vocabulary glossary, selected reader reflections and closing reflection. Reader reviews included entries and excerpts. AI summary is optional and editable. Photos are excluded from export by default. General data export and account/data deletion are required.

## Shared journal gallery - MVP

Every journal starts private. A reader previews and explicitly publishes selected entries, during reading or after finishing. Recommended initial audience: all signed-in members, not anonymous internet visitors; confirm this default before implementing access policies.

A publication is a separate versioned snapshot: later private edits or new entries do not automatically appear. Updating requires another deliberate publish action. Support unpublish, reports and administrator removal. Unpublish revokes platform access but cannot recall copies already saved by another person.

Group journals by a canonical book/work, with translator/edition differences visible. Browse/filter by book, author and reading status, show spoiler/progress notices, and open the shared journal. A book may initially have no shared journals; never fabricate community content. Comments, reactions, messaging and following are later scope.

## Navigation

Proposed: Library / Journal / Gallery, with prominent Ask and New post actions inside a book. If no book is active, Journal offers to open or add one. Confirm exact navigation in the visual review.

## Later

Textbooks and research-paper workflows, cross-book memory, flashcards, additional templates, print ordering, groups and education features. No placeholder mode picker in onboarding.
